How to Play & Rules

Chess Master supports full standard chess rules including piece movements, check detection, checkmate, stalemate, castling, pawn promotion, and en passant.

  • Movement: Click any piece to see its legal destination dots. Click a dot to move.
  • Castling: Move your King two squares towards the Rook to castle (if paths are clear and rights preserved).
  • Pawn Promotion: Reaching the final rank prompts a choice for Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
  • En Passant: Pawns can capture opposing pawns that move two squares forward on the previous turn.
